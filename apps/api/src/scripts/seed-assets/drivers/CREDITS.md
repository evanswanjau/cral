# Driver photo credits

Photos in `male/` and `female/` here are used by `npm run seed:drivers -w apps/api`
(PR DR3, `docs/plans/drivers-that-work.md`). All come from Wikimedia Commons
(categories "Men of Kenya", "Women of Kenya" and their "at work" sub-categories)
under the licence named beside each one, downloaded once on 2026-11-27, cropped
to head and shoulders at 640 x 800 and recompressed. Photos whose title names
the person in full were left out, so no public figure stands in for a made-up
driver. Under CC BY and CC BY-SA the author must be credited where a photo is shown.

The names, rates and counties the seed gives these photos are made up; the
people in them are not CRAL drivers. Seeded data never reaches production
(`seed:drivers` refuses it).
