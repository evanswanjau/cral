# Vehicle photo credits

Photos in the `<model>/` folders here are used by `npm run seed:hire -w apps/api`
(PR H2, `docs/plans/hire-that-works.md`). All come from Wikimedia Commons under
the licence named beside each one, downloaded once on 2026-11-26, resized to
1280 px on the long side and recompressed to 150 KB or less. Any number plate
a photo showed was blurred (or was already blanked by the photographer); the
`plates_blurred` count in `_manifest.json` says how many areas were blurred.
Under CC BY and CC BY-SA the author must be credited where a photo is shown.

The flat `*.jpg` files beside the folders belong to `seed:demo`; their sources
are the older entries in `_manifest.json`.
